Owner-confirmed physical Android touch completion and selected larger-text/reduced-motion checks passed, exact scaling tier not measured. No largest-tier/tablet/dark/custom-font blanket claim. Native Android/iOS tree roles and direct accessible actions are supporting implementation evidence only. During TalkBack guidance the owner chose not to enable it; package presence was checked and the Accessibility page opened without toggling services. Do not repeatedly ask them to enable it. Physical iPhone touch and owner-selected larger-text/Reduce Motion checks also passed. Owner explicitly deferred VoiceOver activation; no reader result exists.

Default-palette contrast was calculated using sRGB relative luminance, recorded in evidence/default-contrast.json. Listed pairs exceed 4.5:1. This is a calculation for opaque default pairs, not a full composited/custom-theme/dark-mode audit. Disabled opacity, consumer-rendered content, custom backgrounds/fonts and localization remain integration responsibilities; disabled text exemptions are not used to claim all themes pass.

Severity: **Blocker release gate** for unverified critical non-gesture completion/focus/speech until physically tested or appropriately mitigated. No reproduced reader failure is invented. Arrow defect was Medium visual and corrected/owner-confirmed. English default announcements/hints and app-supplied labels/theme fit are Medium integration scope. Manual reader enable/navigation/exit instructions and per-component steps are in docs/release/physical-device-testing.md; record Passed/Failed/Not Tested and observer, not merely a semantics checklist.
