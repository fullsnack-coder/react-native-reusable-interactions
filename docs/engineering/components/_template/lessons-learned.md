# Lessons learned: Name

Complete after implementation or an abandoned investigation. Do not fill it from expectations alone.

## What worked

Observed decisions or techniques, with evidence links.

## What failed or changed

Observed failures, rejected approaches, and why the plan changed.

## Accessibility and platform findings

Actual Android and iOS results, including reduced motion and alternative interaction checks.

## Performance findings

Link measured results. If no measurement was performed, say **not measured**.

## Follow-up

Open issues, technical debt, and changes recommended for future components or ADRs.
